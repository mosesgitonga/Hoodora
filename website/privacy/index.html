Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Hoodora collects when you use the Hoodora app and this website, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Hoodora is the data controller. For anything about your data, email privacy@hoodora.one.

The short version

  • Neighbours see the name of your area, never your exact location. We keep no history of where you've been.
  • We keep only an ID for your Google account, not your email address, Google name or photo.
  • Your phone number is never shown to other people.
  • We don't sell your personal data, and Hoodora shows no ads from ad networks.
  • You can download all your data, or delete your account, from the app at any time.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Google account ID. You sign in with Google. We keep only Google's ID for your account, not your email address, name or photo, although Google sends them to the app.
  • Profile: the display name you choose, and anything you add, such as a bio, profile photo or cover photo.
  • Confirmations and consents: that you are 18 or older, and your agreement to our terms, this policy and the use of your location, each with the date and the version you agreed to. Marketing consent is optional, and you can withdraw it.
  • Phone number, only if you add one. You need one to post jobs and to earn the verified-neighbour badge. It is checked by an SMS code sent by Google Firebase.

What you create

  • Posts, stories (gone after 24 hours), comments, reactions and votes.
  • Polls. When you vote, we record only that you voted, so you can't vote twice, and add one to that answer's count. Which answer you chose is never stored, so nobody, us included, can tell how you voted.
  • Views. We count how many people saw a post, without keeping who: the count can't name anyone.
  • Marketplace listings, service and job posts, reviews, events, replies to events, and groups.
  • Photos. We remove the location and camera details stored inside photos, and delete the original file as soon as it has been processed.
  • Messages, stored so they can be delivered to the people you chat with. Staff don't read messages. A moderator sees a message only if someone in that chat reports it.
  • Your Circle (neighbours you know), who you follow, and who you block.

Your devices and sign-ins

  • Your sign-in sessions, with the device type and name, so you can see where you're signed in and sign out remotely.
  • A random ID the app creates when installed. We store it only in a scrambled (hashed) form, so that a ban for serious abuse can reach the phones an account used.
  • A push token, so we can send you notifications. Your notification settings and notifications are kept too.

Safety records

  • Reports you make and their outcome; reports about you and what moderators decided. We never tell anyone who reported them.
  • Warnings, restrictions, suspensions and bans. A banned phone number, Google account or device is kept only in scrambled form.

Technical data

  • When your phone or browser talks to our servers, we log the IP address, the time, and which part of the service was used. We don't log your messages, phone number, location or sign-in tokens.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

2. Your location

Location is what makes Hoodora local, so we have designed it to keep as little as possible:

  • Your home area. When you set your home, we keep an approximate area about a few hundred metres across. We don't keep the exact point your phone reported. Moving your home replaces the old one; there is no history.
  • Your area is shown only by name. Other people see only an area name and a rough distance band, such as "1–3 km". The area is always large enough to include many people, so you can't be singled out.
  • Verified neighbour. To award the badge, we keep a yes or no for each of the last 14 days, saying whether your phone was in your home area. We don't keep where it was.
  • Where you can post. To stop people posting to areas they don't live in, we keep a yes or no per area per day, for 7 days, then delete it.
  • When you're away from home. If you look around a different area, we hold that area for about two hours, in memory only, so you see what's near you. It is never saved to our database.
  • Counts, never names. Each hour we count how many app users are in each area, to plan and run the service. The counts name nobody, and are kept for one year.

Location use is based on your consent, which you give when you sign up. Without it, Hoodora can't show you anything nearby.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the service: show you posts, listings, jobs, services and events within your radius, deliver messages and notifications, and let you manage your account.
  • To keep people safe: the verified-neighbour badge, reviews, reports and moderation, limits on new accounts, and protection against job scams and spam.
  • To keep the service working and secure: fixing problems, preventing abuse, and protecting accounts.
  • To understand how Hoodora is used, from counts that identify nobody.
  • To send marketing, only if you choose to receive it.

We process your data because you asked us to provide the service (a contract), with your consent (location and marketing), and where the law requires it.

4. What other people see

  • Your display name, profile photo, bio and any other profile details you add.
  • Your area name and a rough distance band, never your exact location.
  • Your verified-neighbour badge, rating and reviews, and what you post.
  • Your messages, only to the people in that chat.

Your phone number and Google account are never shown to other users.

8. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used, which this policy does.
  • Access your data, and take a copy. In the app: Settings › Your data › Get my data.
  • Correct it. Edit your profile in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete it. In the app, or by email. See Delete your Hoodora account.
  • Object, or withdraw consent, for example to marketing, in the app's Privacy and consent settings, at any time.
  • Restrict how it is processed, and move it elsewhere: the copy from Get my data is a file you can keep.

To use any of these by email, write to privacy@hoodora.one. We may ask you to confirm details that only the account holder would know, so nobody can act on someone else's account. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. How we protect it

  • All connections to Hoodora are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only in scrambled form, and every sign-in can be ended from the app.
  • Only a small number of staff can see account data, after signing in to a separate staff system. Their actions are recorded.
  • Chats and the feed saved on your phone for offline reading are encrypted, and erased when you sign out.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner, as the law requires.

10. Age limit

Hoodora is for adults aged 18 and over. You confirm your age when you sign up. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they apply.
