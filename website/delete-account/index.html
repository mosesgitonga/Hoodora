Delete your Hoodora account

Applies to the Hoodora app, by Hoodora

You can delete your Hoodora account and the data that goes with it at any time, in the app or by email. Deleting is free, and you don't need to give a reason.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Hoodora, tap Me, then the Settings icon.
  2. Tap Your data, and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account, read what happens, tick the box to confirm, and tap Delete my account.

Want a copy of your data first? On the same screen, tap Get my data before you delete.

Without the app: ask by email

If you no longer have the app, or can't sign in, email privacy@hoodora.one with the subject "Delete my Hoodora account". Include:

  • the display name you use on Hoodora;
  • the email address of the Google account you sign in with;
  • your phone number, if you added one to Hoodora.

We'll reply to confirm. Before deleting, we may ask for details only the account holder would know, so nobody can delete someone else's account. Once confirmed, your account is deleted the same way as from the app.

What happens next

  • Straight away: you're signed out on every phone. Nobody can message you, add you to a group or invite you to their Circle, and you no longer appear in People nearby.
  • For 30 days your account waits, in case you change your mind. Sign in again within that time to cancel.
  • After 30 days it is deleted for good.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, photos, bio, Google account ID and phone number.
  • Your posts, stories, comments and reactions.
  • Your marketplace listings, service and job posts, reviews and events.
  • The text and photos of messages you sent. In other people's chats, they show as "message deleted".
  • Your home area, group memberships, Circle, follows and blocks.
  • Your devices, sign-ins, notifications and settings.
