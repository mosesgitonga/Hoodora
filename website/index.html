Features

Everything happening in your neighbourhood, in one app

How it works

Your own radius keeps it local

Every Hoodora user has a radius that adjusts to how many neighbours are around. In a busy estate it shrinks, so your feed stays truly local. In a quieter area it widens, so there is always something to see.

  1. Sign in with Google

    No passwords and no codes to wait for. Pick a display name and you're in.

  2. Set your home area

    Neighbours only ever see the name of your area, never your exact location.

  3. See what's happening around you

    Posts, listings, jobs and events from within your radius, with filters for how far you want to look.

Safety and privacy

Safe by design, for every neighbour

Hoodora is built around Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and around the scams people actually meet.

Your location stays private

Exact locations are never shown. You appear only as part of an area big enough that you can't be singled out.

Job-scam protection

Posting a job needs a verified phone number, posts that ask for fees are held for review, and job chats remind everyone: never pay to get a job.

Report and block in one tap

Message requests keep strangers out of your chats, and anyone can report or block a person. Real moderators review what's reported.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Hoodora free?

Yes. Hoodora is free to download and free to use: posting, selling, chatting, joining groups and going to events cost nothing.

Does Hoodora show my exact location?

No. Neighbours only ever see an area name, never your exact location or your street. You choose your home area when you sign up.

Who can see what I post?

People within your radius: neighbours in your area. You can also post to your Circle, the neighbours you know, and to groups you belong to.

How does Hoodora keep job posts safe?

Posting a job needs a verified phone number, posts that ask for fees are held for review, and every job chat carries a reminder never to pay to get a job. Anyone can report or block a person in one tap.

How do I sign up?

Sign in with your Google account, choose a display name, and set your home area. A phone number is only needed if you post jobs or want the verified-neighbour badge, and it is checked by SMS.

Is Hoodora on iPhone?

Hoodora is on Android first, where most of Kenya is. An iPhone version is planned.

Where is Hoodora available?

Hoodora works anywhere in Kenya. Your radius widens where there are fewer neighbours, so there is always something to see.

How is Hoodora different from WhatsApp groups?

Instead of joining dozens of groups, you get one place for everything nearby: news, questions, alerts, things for sale, jobs, services and events, sorted by how close they are, with reviews and safety tools built in.
